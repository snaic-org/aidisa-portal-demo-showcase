How AIDISA handles a piece of feedback

  1. Receive: feedback arrives from IFS, web forms or social media and joins a queue.
  2. Process: a worker runs pre-processing, the severity model, keyword/NER extraction and category classification.
  3. Assign: the case is routed to a team and skill, then given to the least-loaded available staff member.
  4. Detect bursts: keyword counts are compared against their recent baseline, and spikes raise an alert.
